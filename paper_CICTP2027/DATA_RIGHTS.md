# Attribution and distribution scope

Published numeric records are the authors' simulation outcomes, experiment
definitions and derived analyses. The manuscript/source publication is authorized
by the project owner. Existing repository code, figure assets and derived local
geometry inputs are retained with their source lineage and notices. No new blanket
license is asserted for third-party material; the ASCE template retains its
original rights and existing upstream terms continue to apply.

Raw municipal GIS archives, downloaded map collections and third-party full-text
articles are not redistributed in this release. The supplied geometry files are
the idealized/local simulation inputs already used and published in this project,
not a replacement for the source GIS datasets. Consult the existing source
attribution records and the respective city/open-data providers before obtaining
or redistributing original data. Do not infer a new license for those inputs.

The 1,920 raw trajectory/XML directories remain local (about 13.6 GB). Compact
derived evidence, scalar outcomes, job specifications, configuration hashes and
rerun scripts are published instead. Developer home paths/run-directory columns,
the full private absolute-path run manifest and credential/environment files are
withheld. No credentials or restricted source GIS are part of the research package.

`INPUT_SOURCES.csv` records source and export SHA256 values. Exports that remove
filesystem metadata are explicitly marked; scientific counters/results are
retained. The immutable replication-selection CSV is copied byte-for-byte so its
original freeze hash remains verifiable. Old raw XML that was deleted before this
extension cannot be recreated as historical insertion evidence.
